Stock-Out & Depletion Estimation

Estimate item depletion by comparing the time since the last insertion with the item's historical replenishment pattern.

Learn each item's typical insertion frequency and interval between insertions.
Identify when the current gap significantly exceeds its normal pattern.
Use this as an indicator of potential depletion or stock-out.

Example:
Coca-Cola is typically inserted every 3–4 days. If no insertion is detected for 7+ days, the system can flag Coca-Cola as likely depleted and trigger a personalized replenishment recommendation.

Key principle: Identify items that are expected but missing, rather than treating every absence as a stock-out.
